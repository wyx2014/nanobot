# Corporate Presentation Guidance

## Content

- Give every slide one conclusion. Use the title to state the topic and the body to support it.
- Prefer 3-6 bullets, each under 45 Chinese characters when possible.
- Keep units, reporting periods and comparison bases explicit.
- Put uncertain or missing data in the outline for confirmation; do not fabricate values.
- Cite internal reports or approved public sources in `footer` or nearby body text when necessary.

## Layout Selection

- Start from the page's reader task and main exhibit, not from a favorite layout.
- `metrics`: a small set of comparable headline numbers.
- `chart`: a verified numeric relationship with source, period, unit and comparison basis.
- `table`: exact values or comparisons that readers need to scan across dimensions.
- `timeline`: genuine chronological evolution with three to eight milestones.
- `process`: an ordered operating or delivery sequence with three to six stages.
- `image-grid`: two to six related evidence images, screenshots or states.
- `comparison`: two alternatives, periods, products or before/after states.
- `two-column`: parallel workstreams or completed work versus next steps.
- `image-text`: a real product, interface, project site or other evidence image plus explanation.
- `section`: a genuine chapter transition, not an ordinary content slide.
- `title-body`: narrative findings that do not benefit from a more specific visual structure.
- Avoid three consecutive body pages with the same layout. For decks of six or more content pages,
  use visual or relationship layouts on roughly one third or more unless the material is inherently
  textual.

## Visual Evidence

- Decide the required visual subjects before final page design and prepare them in a batch.
- Prefer user-provided or approved internal assets. For public topics, prefer official sources and
  retain the owning-page attribution.
- One image should support one conclusion. Do not repeat the same generic visual across chapters.
- Generated images express concepts or atmosphere; they do not prove historical events, product
  capabilities, project completion or financial results.
- Do not create numeric charts from subjective scores or invented maturity indexes merely to make a
  page look visual.

## Quality Gate

Before delivery, verify:

- The PPTX opens and the slide count matches the deck specification.
- The original cover, body branding and closing page are present.
- No placeholder prompts, missing images, external relationships, macros or embedded objects remain.
- Titles do not collide with the logo; content stays inside the blue-green frame.
- Text, tables and charts remain editable in PowerPoint or WPS.
- Distinct images are not repeatedly substituted for a real media strategy.
- Every external visual and every numeric chart has a source label.
